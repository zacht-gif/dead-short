About

Wired is a circuit-wiring puzzle that never rolls a die. Every spark's path, every board, every tick of the clock is fixed and repeatable — the only variable in the room is you. Connect the pairs, dodge the current, beat your own best time. No luck to blame for a short, none to thank for a clean run — just better routing and a faster finish.

How to play

Drag from a dot to its matching color — or tap your way there cell by cell — to wire every pair together; no two paths are allowed to cross. Sparks patrol fixed, repeating tracks whose route is always drawn on the board — nothing here is random, and Trace will show you exactly where every spark lands one, two and three seconds from now, free and as often as you like. Touch an unfinished wire with a spark and it shorts out for a time penalty; once a wire is finished it's insulated, and safe for good. Your score is solve time plus a penalty for every short, so precision counts as much as speed. The clock doesn't start until your first move — so plan all you want, then go fast.
