⚡ Dead Short

Route every pair. Time the current. Nothing is random.
How to play
The board only moves when you do. Drag from a colored dot toward its matching dot to lay a route. Every cell you draw is one move, and every move steps the whole board once: the current flows one cell further along the wire you are working on, and every spark takes one step of its patrol. Between moves nothing happens at all, so take as long as you like.

Only one wire carries current at a time, so switching to another color costs a move — and the half-built wire you left behind is still live. If a spark reaches it, that wire shorts out and its current is lost. Your drawn route survives; only the current does, and the short adds 5 to your score. Finished wires are insulated for good and are solid: nothing can cross them or the components on the board, so the order you run them in decides what is still possible.

Wait spends a move without moving the current, which is how you let a spark go by. Trace is free and shows where every spark will be for the next three moves. Your score is simply the moves you spent, so the shortest route is not always the cheapest one.

About

Dead Short is a turn-based circuit-wiring puzzle that never rolls a die. Nothing on the board moves until you move, and then everything moves exactly one step: every spark's path, every board, every consequence is fixed and repeatable, and the only variable in the room is you. Connect the pairs, time the current, beat your own best. No luck to blame for a short, none to thank for a clean run — just better routing and fewer moves.

How to play

Drag from a dot to its matching color — or tap your way there cell by cell — to lay out that pair's route. One cell is one move, and a move steps the entire board: the current flows one cell further, and every spark takes one step of its patrol. Only one wire carries current at a time, so switching to another color costs a move of its own. No two routes may cross, and neither may pass through a component soldered to the board, so the order you run them in decides what is still reachable.

Sparks patrol fixed, repeating tracks that are always drawn on the board, and the cell a spark occupies right now is outlined — that outline is the hitbox, exactly. Let live current touch it and that wire shorts out for a 5-move penalty; your drawn route survives, so it simply starts filling again. A finished wire is insulated and safe for good. Nothing here is random, and nothing is rushed: Wait spends a move and lets a spark go past, Trace shows you exactly where every spark will be for the next three moves, and between two moves the board holds still for as long as you want it to.

Accessibility

Colorblind Mode
Recolors and reshapes wire pairs so every color reads by more than hue alone.
Reduce Motion
Softens the spark's pulsing glow and turns off the title screen's intro animation.
Mute Sound
Turns off all game sound effects.

Practice

Untimed practice
Off by default. Turns the clock off entirely: the board advances one move per action and nothing moves while you think, which is how a level can be learned without racing it. Nothing is recorded — no time, no medal, no daily streak, no cosmetic unlock — so a practice run can never stand in for a played one.
