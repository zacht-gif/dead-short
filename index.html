About

Wired is a circuit-wiring puzzle that never rolls a die. Every spark's path, every board, every tick of the clock is fixed and repeatable — the only variable in the room is you. Connect the pairs, dodge the current, beat your own best time. No luck to blame for a short, none to thank for a clean run — just better routing and a faster finish.

How to play

Drag from a dot to its matching color — or tap your way there cell by cell — to lay out that pair's route. Drawing costs nothing and happens instantly. Current is the slow part: it flows one cell per tick, down one route at a time, and when a wire completes the current moves to the next route you've drawn. No two routes may cross, and neither may pass through a component soldered to the board, so the order things energize decides what's still reachable.

Sparks patrol fixed, repeating tracks that are always drawn on the board, and the cell a spark occupies right now is outlined — that outline is the hitbox, exactly. Let live current touch it and that wire shorts out for a time penalty; your drawn route survives, so it simply starts filling again. A finished wire is insulated and safe for good. Nothing here is random: Trace shows you precisely where every spark lands one, two and three seconds from now, free and as often as you like, and the board stays frozen until your first move.
